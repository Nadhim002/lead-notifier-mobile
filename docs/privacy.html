Lead Notifier is published by Mohammed Nadhim, an individual developer. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), and comparable laws elsewhere, Mohammed Nadhim is the party responsible for the personal data described below.

Contact: regentbagsown@gmail.com

2. What this app is

Lead Notifier delivers real-time alerts to your phone when a new sales lead is purchased on your linked account. Leads are captured by the companion Lead Notifier browser extension on your computer; this Android app receives the alert and displays the lead so you can respond quickly.

This policy covers the Android app only. The browser extension is covered by its own policy.

3.2 Data about your leads (third-party personal data)

The app displays sales leads belonging to your business. A lead may contain:

  • Buyer name
  • Buyer mobile number
  • City and state
  • Product/enquiry title, quantity, and price
  • Timestamp

Important: this information is about your customers, not about you. You are the party responsible for it; Lead Notifier processes it on your behalf, solely to display it to you and alert you. We do not use lead data for any purpose of our own, do not sell it, do not use it for advertising, profiling, or training any model, and do not disclose it to anyone other than the service providers listed in section 5.

You are responsible for ensuring you have a lawful basis to collect and process your buyers' personal data.

4. How your data is used

We use the data in section 3.1 only to:

  1. Sign you in and identify your account
  2. Deliver lead alerts to the correct phone
  3. Enforce your subscription and device-count limits
  4. Let you name, view, and remove your registered phones
  5. Diagnose faults and prevent abuse of the service

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

6. Where your data is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase in the asia-southeast1 (Singapore) region. Push notifications are additionally routed through Expo's infrastructure, which may process data in other countries, including the United States.

If you are in India, this means your data — and your leads' data — is transferred outside India. By using the app you acknowledge this transfer.

7. How long we keep it

Lead records and account data are retained until you delete them. We do not automatically delete leads after a fixed period. You control removal (see section 9).

If you stop using the app without deleting your account, your data remains stored until you request deletion.

8. When we may disclose data

We may disclose data only where legally required — for example, in response to a valid legal demand, court order, or where necessary to establish or defend legal claims, or to prevent fraud, abuse, or harm.

9. Your choices and rights

Remove a phone. Settings → My Phones → remove any device. This deletes that device's record and push token immediately, and it will stop receiving alerts.

Sign out. Settings → Sign Out. This ends the session on this phone. It does not delete your data.

Delete your account and all data. Account deletion is performed through the Lead Notifier browser extension on your computer, which removes your account record, lead history, and registered devices.

If you cannot access the extension, email regentbagsown@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account" from the address you signed in with. We will delete your account data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, or obtain a copy of your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. In India, you may raise a grievance with us at regentbagsown@gmail.com and, if unsatisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address above.

10. Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS.
  • Data at rest in Firebase is encrypted by Google.
  • Database rules restrict each account so that it can read and write only its own records; one customer cannot read another customer's leads or devices.
  • Subscription records cannot be modified by users.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

11. Children

Lead Notifier is a business tool intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact regentbagsown@gmail.com and we will delete it.
